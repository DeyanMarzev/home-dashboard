Metered charges

Having a water meter means we base your bill on the amount of water you use. This is the fairest way to pay. Plus, you can easily track your water usage in your online account.

We’ll send you a bill every six months. Your bill will show charges for every cubic metre (m3) of water used.

One cubic metre equals 1,000 litres which is an estimated 25 quick showers. It costs £2.7346 per m3 for clean water and £1.4721 per m3 for wastewater as of 1 April 2026.

If you're a metered customer and pay your bill by instalments, your payments may have increased. We’ve created a guide to help you understand regular payments.
